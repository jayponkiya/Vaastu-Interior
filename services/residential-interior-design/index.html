Residential interior design

Residential interior design in Ahmedabad, for homes that work as hard as you do

From a first 2BHK to a family bungalow, we plan every room around how you live, show it to you in 3D and build it with our own team.

01 Overview

Residential Interior Design in Ahmedabad

A home in Ahmedabad has to cope with a lot: long, hot summers, dust that finds every shelf, monsoon damp, guests who arrive unannounced and often three generations under one roof. Good residential interior design starts there, not with a mood board. We look at how mornings run in your house, where shoes and school bags pile up, which room catches the harsh western sun and how much storage you really need.

Only then do we bring in the look you want, whether that is warm wood and soft neutrals, a clean contemporary home or something more traditional. Every room is drawn to scale, shown to you in photo-real 3D and built by our team, so the home you move into is the one you approved on screen.

04 Our process

How this service runs

The same six-step process sits behind every project. Here is how it plays out for this service.

  1. 01

    Walk-through and brief

    We visit, measure every room and understand your family routines, your Vastu preferences and the budget you want to work within.

  2. 02

    Room-by-room layouts

    Scaled plans fix furniture, storage and circulation for each room before any colours or finishes are chosen.

  3. 03

    3D views and samples

    You see each room in 3D with real laminates, fabrics and lights, and change anything until it feels like home.

  4. 04

    Build and handover

    Our team executes to the drawings, we close the snag list together and deep clean before you move in.

06 FAQ

Questions about residential interior design

More answers on our FAQ page.

Can you design just a few rooms rather than the whole flat?

Yes. Many clients start with the living room and kitchen, or the master bedroom, and do the rest later. We plan the first rooms so they fit the rest of the home when you are ready.

Do you design homes in line with Vastu?

If you want it, yes. We can place the entrance, kitchen and cooking direction, master bedroom, pooja space and heavy storage according to Vastu principles, and show you any trade-offs against daily use before you decide.

We are moving into a new flat. When should we contact you?

As early as you can, ideally before possession. That gives time to measure, plan the layouts and finalise the 3D design, so site work can start soon after you get the keys.

Will the final home really look like the 3D views?

That is the point of them. The 3D views use the actual materials and fixtures you approve, and our working drawings take the same details to site, so the result matches what you saw.

Can you work with furniture we already own?

Yes. We measure the pieces you want to keep and plan the new layout, colours and lighting around them so they look intended.
