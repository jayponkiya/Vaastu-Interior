Architectural & technical drawings

Architectural and technical drawings for interiors in Ahmedabad

Clear 2D layouts, working drawings and sections, so carpenters, electricians and contractors build exactly what was designed.

01 Overview

Architectural and Technical Drawings in Ahmedabad

A beautiful design only becomes a beautiful room if the people on site know exactly what to build. Vague drawings lead to guesswork: a switch in the wrong place, a ceiling a few inches too low or a wardrobe that does not clear the door. Each of those is cheap to fix on paper and expensive to fix in plaster and plywood.

Our drawings are measured, dimensioned and drawn for the trade that will use them. Carpenters get joinery details, electricians get point and circuit layouts, and the false ceiling team gets levels and light positions. We prepare full sets for our own projects and also as a standalone service for homeowners, architects and contractors who need dependable drawings.

04 Our process

How this service runs

The same six-step process sits behind every project. Here is how it plays out for this service.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We measure the site, record existing services and note anything that affects the design.

  2. 02

    Base drawings

    An accurate base plan and layouts are prepared and checked against the site.

  3. 03

    Detailing

    Working drawings, sections and service layouts are developed for each trade.

  4. 04

    Issue and support

    Drawings are issued for construction and we answer site queries as work proceeds.

06 FAQ

Questions about architectural & technical drawings

More answers on our FAQ page.

Can I order drawings only, without design or execution?

Yes. We prepare drawing sets as a standalone service for homeowners, architects and contractors who need accurate, buildable drawings.

What formats do you provide?

Drawings are shared as print-ready PDFs at standard sheet sizes, and in editable CAD format where agreed.

Do your drawings include electrical layouts?

Yes. We prepare switch, socket and lighting layouts coordinated with the furniture plan, along with false ceiling plans showing levels and light positions.

Can you draw an existing home that has no plans?

Yes. We measure the home and prepare accurate as-built drawings, which are a useful starting point for any renovation.
