Modular kitchens & wardrobes

Modular kitchens, wardrobes and custom furniture in Ahmedabad

Storage that fits your space to the millimetre, finishes that cope with daily use and hardware that keeps working for years.

01 Overview

Modular Kitchen and Wardrobe in Ahmedabad

The kitchen and the wardrobes are the parts of a home you use most and the first to show wear. An Indian kitchen has to handle daily tadka, heavy utensils, masala dabbas and steel vessels, a water purifier and often a separate wash area. Wardrobes have to hold far more than clothes: bedding, suitcases, documents and festival things that come out once a year.

We design modular units around those needs. Every cabinet is measured on site, planned on drawings and shown in 3D, so you know where each drawer, basket and shelf goes before production starts. We suggest moisture-resistant boards where there is water, durable laminates or veneers on surfaces you touch daily, and soft-close hinges and channels you will not have to replace.

06 FAQ

Questions about modular kitchens & wardrobes

More answers on our FAQ page.

Which board is best for a modular kitchen?

Under the sink and near water we recommend moisture-resistant boards. For the rest of the kitchen and wardrobes we suggest the right board for the load and budget, and every material is named in your estimate so you know exactly what you are getting.

Can you redo an old kitchen without breaking the platform?

Often, yes. If the existing granite platform and plumbing are in good condition we can design new modular units around them, which saves time and cost.

Do you install appliances such as hobs and chimneys?

We plan the cabinets, cut-outs and electrical points for your appliances and coordinate their installation, so everything fits and works together.

How do you plan wardrobe interiors?

We go through what each person stores, from saris and suits to bags and bedding, and divide the wardrobe into hanging, shelves, drawers and lofts to suit.
