Estimation & project management

Interior estimation and project management in Ahmedabad

Know what every room will cost before work begins, and keep the project on track and on budget once it does.

01 Overview

Interior Cost Estimation and Project Management in Ahmedabad

Interior budgets usually go wrong for two reasons: the first quote was a lump sum nobody could check, and changes on site were never priced before they were made. Both are avoidable. An itemised bill of quantities tied to the approved drawings shows exactly what is being built and what each part costs.

We prepare that estimate room by room and material by material, so you can see where the money goes and decide where to save without weakening the design. Once work starts, we coordinate vendors and trades, track progress against the schedule and price any change before it happens, so the final bill does not come as a surprise.

06 FAQ

Questions about estimation & project management

More answers on our FAQ page.

What is a BOQ and why do I need one?

A bill of quantities lists every item in the project with its size, specification and quantity. It lets you compare quotes fairly and stops costs from drifting during the work.

Can you prepare an estimate for a design done by someone else?

Yes, if the drawings and specifications are complete. We can also point out missing details that would otherwise turn into extra costs later.

How do you handle changes during the project?

Every change is priced and agreed before it is carried out, so you always know its effect on the budget.

Do you offer project management only?

Yes. We can coordinate vendors and trades, track progress and report to you on a project where you have engaged the contractors directly.
