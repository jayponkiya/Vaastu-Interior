Space planning & consultation

Space planning and interior design consultation in Ahmedabad

Get the layout right first. Expert advice on how to use your space before you spend on furniture, finishes or construction.

01 Overview

Space Planning and Interior Design Consultation in Ahmedabad

The most expensive mistakes in an interior project are made before any money is spent: a sofa that blocks the balcony door, a dining table squeezed into a corridor, a bedroom with nowhere sensible for the wardrobe. Space planning solves these on paper, where changes cost nothing.

Whether you have just booked a flat, are planning a renovation or simply feel your home does not work, a consultation gives you a clear plan. We study the space, listen to how you want to use it and propose layouts for furniture, storage and circulation. If Vastu matters to you, we show how each option sits against it. You can take the plan forward with us or on your own.

The same six-step process sits behind every project. Here is how it plays out for this service.

    Listen

    We talk through how you use the space, what frustrates you and what you want to change.

  2. 02

    Study

    We review the plan or visit the site and note light, ventilation, services and constraints.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Decide

    We refine the chosen plan and give you a prioritised list of next steps.

06 FAQ

Questions about space planning & consultation

More answers on our FAQ page.

Is a consultation useful if I want to do the interiors myself?

Very. A good layout and clear direction help you buy the right furniture and brief any contractor, even if you manage the rest yourself.

Can you plan from a builder's floor plan before possession?

Yes. We can work from the floor plan and confirm the measurements on site once you get possession.

Do you consider Vastu in space planning?

If you want us to, yes. We show how each layout option sits against Vastu principles so you can choose with the trade-offs in front of you.

What do I get at the end of a consultation?

Scaled layout drawings for the agreed rooms and a written list of recommendations you can act on, with us or on your own.
