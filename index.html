02 About the studio

We design around the way you live, then we stay on site until it is built that way.

Vastu Interior is an interior design studio on SG Highway in Bodakdev. We work on apartments, bungalows, offices and showrooms across Ahmedabad, and we plan for the city as it is: strong western sun, long dusty summers, joint families and rooms that have to do more than one job.

Every project starts with listening and measuring, moves through drawings and 3D views you can change, and ends with our own team on site. One studio, one estimate, one person to call.

More about the studio
  1. 01

    Listen first

    We start with how you live or work, your routines and your budget, not with a catalogue.

  2. 02

    Show before we build

    Drawings and 3D views you approve before a single cabinet is ordered.

  3. 03

    Stay until it is done

    Our own team on site, through the snag list and the final clean.

03 Services

Eight services, one team

Take one service on its own, or let us run the whole project from the first measured drawing to the final clean.

  1. 01 Residential interior design Apartments, villas and bungalows planned room by room, from furniture layout to lighting.
  2. 02 Commercial interior design Offices, clinics and restaurants planned around how your people and customers move.
  3. 03 Modular kitchens & wardrobes Kitchens, wardrobes and storage built to your measurements, in finishes that cope with daily use.
  4. 04 3D visualisation & rendering Photo-real views of every room, so you can change a colour or a cabinet before anything is ordered.
  5. 05 Architectural & technical drawings Measured plans, elevations and electrical and ceiling layouts your site team can build from.
  6. 06 Turnkey interior execution Civil changes, carpentry, electrical, painting and finishing, handled by one team on one schedule.
  7. 07 Estimation & project management An itemised estimate before work starts, then site visits and updates until the last snag is closed.
  8. 08 Space planning & consultation A second opinion, a furniture layout or a full room-by-room plan, before you spend on anything else.

05 Process

Six steps from first visit to handover

You always know what happens next, what it costs and what we need from you.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit, measure and listen: who lives or works here, what is not working today and what the budget allows.

  2. 02

    Space planning

    Furniture layouts and circulation, drawn to scale, so every room has a clear purpose before style comes in.

  3. 03

    3D design

    Photo-real views of each room with real materials. You change what you want until it feels right.

  4. 04

    Estimation

    An itemised estimate tied to the approved design, so you know the cost of every cabinet and finish.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Our carpenters, electricians and painters work to the drawings, with regular site visits and updates.

  6. 06

    Handover

    A full walk-through, a snag list closed item by item, then a deep clean before we hand you the keys.
