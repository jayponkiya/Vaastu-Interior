Questions we hear often

How long does a 3BHK interior take in Ahmedabad?

Design and 3D approval usually take three to four weeks. Site work for a full 3BHK, including modular furniture, typically runs ten to fourteen weeks depending on civil changes and material lead times.

Do I see the design before work starts?

Yes. You get a furniture layout, 3D views of each room and a material board. Work on site starts only after you sign off on all three.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Often, yes. We measure what you want to keep and plan the new pieces, colours and lighting around it.
