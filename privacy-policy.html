01 Who we are

This website is run by Vastu Interior, an interior design studio in Ahmedabad. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Vastu Interior, and "you" means anyone who visits the website or contacts us through it.

If you have any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at Vastu Interior, Office 305, Silver Oak Arcade, SG Highway, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380054. Email vastuinterior10@gmail.com, phone +91 63590 31530.

02 What we collect

We only collect personal details that you choose to give us. When you use a form on this website, call us, message us on WhatsApp or email us, this can include:

  • your name, mobile number and email address
  • the area or city where your property is
  • details about your project, such as property type, size, budget range, timeline and any notes you add

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or passwords through this website.

03 How the forms work

Our forms do not store anything on this website. When you press send, your answers are placed into a WhatsApp message to our studio number, and WhatsApp opens so you can review the message and send it yourself. If you choose email instead, the same text is placed in an email from your own mail app.

Once you send it, your message is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own privacy policies, and it reaches our team.

04 How we use your details

We use the details you share only to:

  • reply to your enquiry and arrange a call or site visit
  • prepare layouts, designs, estimates and proposals you ask for
  • carry out and support a project if you go ahead with us
  • keep basic records required for accounts and tax

We do not sell or rent your personal details, and we do not use them for unrelated marketing.

05 Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores two small notes in your own browser for the current visit only (session storage): one so the loading screen is not shown again on every page, and one so the thank-you page can offer a WhatsApp or email link after you send a form. Both are cleared when you close the tab.

Our contact page shows an embedded Google Map. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive information such as your IP address, under the Google privacy policy. Fonts and other files on this website are served from our own server.

06 Sharing

We share your details only when it is needed to answer your enquiry or deliver your project, for example with a carpenter, supplier or consultant working on your site, and only the details they need. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

07 How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as the project and any legal or tax obligations require. After that, we delete them or keep them in a form that no longer identifies you.

08 Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct or complete it, or to erase it, and you can withdraw consent you have given. To make a request, email or call us using the details above. We will respond within a reasonable time.

09 Security

We take reasonable care to protect the details you share with us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive documents through WhatsApp or email unless we ask for them for your project.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. This version is effective from 18 November 2026.
