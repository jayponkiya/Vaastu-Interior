About the studio

Modern interiors, planned around how you live and the principles of Vastu

We are an interior design studio on SG Highway in Bodakdev. We design homes, offices and showrooms across Ahmedabad, and we stay on site until they are built the way you approved them.

01 Our story

Good design and Vastu do not have to pull in different directions

Many families in Ahmedabad want a home that feels modern and works hard every day, and that also respects Vastu Shastra. Too often they are told to choose: a designer who ignores Vastu, or a layout that follows it so strictly that the rooms stop working.

Our name says where we stand. We start every plan with daylight, airflow, storage and how your family actually moves through the space. Where you want it, we then bring in Vastu principles: the placement of the entrance, the kitchen and cooking direction, the master bedroom, the pooja space and where heavy storage sits. When a principle and a practical need disagree, we show you the options side by side and let you decide.

The same thinking runs through our residential and commercial work: clean lines, warm materials, finishes that cope with Ahmedabad's sun and dust, and drawings detailed enough that the site builds exactly what you saw in 3D.

02 Vision & mission

What we are working towards

Vision

Homes and workplaces in Ahmedabad that feel calm, work hard and age well

We want every space we design to still feel right ten years later: easy to clean, easy to live in and built from materials that suit the city's climate.

Mission

Take the guesswork and the gaps out of doing up a space

We keep design, estimation and execution in one studio, show you every room in 3D before work starts and give you an itemised estimate you can read, so what you approve is what gets built.

03 Philosophy

Four things we hold on to

They shape every layout we draw, every material we suggest and every conversation on site.

  1. 01

    Function before style

    Storage, light, airflow and circulation are solved first. The look follows the way the space is used.

  2. 02

    Respect for Vastu

    Applied with care where you want it, balanced openly against practical needs, never used to scare.

  3. 03

    Honest materials

    Finishes chosen for Ahmedabad's heat, dust and monsoon, shown to you as real samples before they are ordered.

  4. 04

    No surprises

    Every room in 3D, every cost itemised and one person to call, from the first visit to the final clean.

05 Founder

Er. Rutvik Virani

Founder & Director

Er. Rutvik Virani is the Founder and Director of Vastu Interior, a professional interior design and turnkey execution firm specializing in residential and commercial projects. With over 4 years of experience in the interior design industry, he brings together creative vision, technical expertise and strategic project management to transform spaces into thoughtfully designed, functional and aesthetically refined environments.

Under his leadership, Vastu Interior delivers comprehensive solutions encompassing space planning, interior design, 2D and 3D visualization, technical and working drawings, customized furniture, modular solutions, cost estimation, material selection and complete project execution.

Driven by a commitment to design excellence, quality craftsmanship, innovation and client satisfaction, he focuses on delivering projects with precision, transparency and attention to every detail, ensuring a seamless journey from initial concept to final execution.
