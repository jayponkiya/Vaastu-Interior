How we work

Six clear steps from first visit to handover

You always know what happens next, what it costs and what we need from you. Here is each step in detail, with what you get at the end of it.

  1. 01Consultation
  2. 02Space planning
  3. 033D design
  4. 04Estimation
  5. 05Execution
  6. 06Handover
01

Consultation

It starts with a conversation and a site visit. We measure every room, photograph the existing services and listen to how you live or work: who uses each space, what is not working today, what you want to keep and the budget you are comfortable with. If Vastu matters to you, this is where we note your priorities.

What you get

  • A measured site survey
  • A written brief we both agree on
  • An honest view of what the budget can cover

Your part

  • Share your must-haves and deal-breakers
  • Tell us about any Vastu preferences
  • Give us access to the site for measuring
02

Space planning

Before colours and finishes, we fix the plan. We draw furniture layouts to scale, work out circulation and storage, and check light and airflow room by room. Where Vastu guidance is part of the brief, we show how each option sits against it so you can choose with the trade-offs in front of you.

What you get

  • Scaled furniture layouts for every room
  • Storage and circulation planned in detail
  • Alternative options where the choice is not obvious

Your part

  • Review the layouts with your family or team
  • Ask for changes until the plan feels right
03

3D design

Once the plan is agreed we design the look: materials, colours, lighting and joinery details. You then see every room as photo-real 3D views with the actual laminates, fabrics and fixtures. Change a colour, a handle or a light and see the result before anything is ordered.

What you get

  • Photo-real 3D views of each room
  • A material and finish board with real samples
  • Rounds of revisions until you sign off

Your part

  • Visit the studio to see and touch samples
  • Approve each room before we move on
04

Estimation

With the design approved, we prepare an itemised estimate tied to those exact drawings and materials. Every cabinet, ceiling, light point and finish is listed by room, so you can see where the money goes and choose where to save without losing the design.

What you get

  • An itemised estimate by room and by material
  • A work schedule with clear stages
  • A written agreement before work starts

Your part

  • Go through the estimate line by line with us
  • Confirm the schedule and any changes
05

Execution

Our team builds to the drawings: civil changes, electrical and plumbing, false ceilings, carpentry, modular units, painting and finishing. A site supervisor coordinates every trade and our designers visit regularly to check the work against what you approved. You get updates at each stage.

What you get

  • Working drawings for every trade
  • Regular site visits and progress updates
  • Quality checks at each stage

Your part

  • Keep us posted on any change of mind early
  • Visit the site at agreed milestones
06

Handover

Before we hand over, we walk through every room with you and make a snag list of anything that is not right. We close it item by item, deep clean the space, and hand over the keys with care instructions and the manufacturer warranties for hardware and appliances.

What you get

  • A joint walk-through and written snag list
  • A deep clean before you move in
  • Care guide and manufacturer warranty papers

Your part

  • Walk through with us and flag anything you notice
  • Move in and enjoy the space
