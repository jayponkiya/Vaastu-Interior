Why Vastu Interior

Fewer surprises, from the first drawing to the last screw

Most interior projects go wrong in the gaps between designer, contractor and client. We close those gaps by keeping design, estimation and execution in one studio.

Six reasons clients choose us

Each one is a habit built into our process, not a promise on a brochure.

    You see it before you pay for it

  2. 02

    An estimate you can read

    Itemised by room and by material, tied to the approved drawings, so you can see where the money goes and choose where to save without losing the design.
