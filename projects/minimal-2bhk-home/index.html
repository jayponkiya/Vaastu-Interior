02 The story

From brief to finished space

The brief

A young couple bought their first flat in Gota and wanted it to feel open and quiet after years in a cramped rental. The budget was careful, so every rupee had to go where they would notice it every day.

The approach

We kept the palette to cream, taupe and one warm walnut wall that holds the TV, speakers and storage behind flush shutters. The living and dining areas share one rug-free floor so the room reads as a single space, and cove lighting in a slim false ceiling makes the walls glow in the evening. Furniture was kept low and light so the windows stay visible from the door.

The result

The flat now feels larger than its size. Storage is built into the walls instead of standing in the room, and the money saved on decorative extras went into better hardware and a good mattress-height bed with drawers.
