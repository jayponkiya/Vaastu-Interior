02 The story

From brief to finished space

The brief

A technology company with forty people was moving into a bare floor plate and expected to grow quickly. They needed open workstations for focused work, enough meeting rooms that nobody had to book days ahead, and a reception that would reassure visiting clients.

The approach

We planned the floor in zones: a walnut reception and client meeting room near the lift, a band of glass-walled meeting rooms in the middle and open workstations along the windows where the daylight is best. Power and data run through raised floor boxes, so new desks can be added without breaking anything. Planter-topped partitions give each team a sense of its own space.

The result

The concept seats the current team comfortably with space reserved for growth. Glass keeps the meeting rooms light while felt panels and a fabric ceiling feature soften the noise in the open areas.
