02 The story

From brief to finished space

The brief

A joint family was renovating a two-storey bungalow in Bodakdev. They wanted the house to feel generous and connected to the garden, with formal space for entertaining and private, quiet bedrooms upstairs. Vastu mattered to them, especially for the entrance, the kitchen and the pooja room.

The approach

We opened the living room to double height and lined one wall with timber slats that run up to the ceiling, giving the space warmth and scale. Full-height glazing faces the garden, shaded by a deep overhang so the west light stays soft. The kitchen, pooja room and master bedroom were placed according to Vastu, and we showed the family where that shaped the plan before they signed off.

The result

Marble floors, natural stone feature walls and solid wood furniture give the house a quiet, lasting richness. The dining area now sits between the kitchen and the garden, and the upstairs bedrooms each have their own dressing area and a view of the trees.
