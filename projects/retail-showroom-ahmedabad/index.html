02 The story

From brief to finished space

The brief

A furniture retailer wanted a showroom that felt less like a warehouse and more like a series of homes. Customers should be able to picture a sofa in their own living room, and staff needed somewhere comfortable to sit with them and go through fabrics and finishes.

The approach

We divided the floor into room-like settings, each with its own rug, lighting and backdrop, linked by a clear loop that leads customers past every collection. The shopfront uses vertical timber slats to frame the window display, and track lighting can be re-aimed whenever the range changes. A lounge near the back holds sample books and a coffee counter.

The result

The concept turns a deep, narrow shop into a walk through several homes. Products are easier to compare, the lounge gives staff a natural place to close a sale, and the display settings can be restyled each season without new construction.
