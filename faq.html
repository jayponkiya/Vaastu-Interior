Cost & payments

How much does interior design cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on the area, the scope of work and the materials you choose. A modular kitchen alone, a full 3BHK and a turnkey office are very different budgets. After the site visit and design, we give you an itemised estimate by room and by material, so you can see the cost of every item and decide where to spend and where to save. The quote itself is free.

Do you charge a separate design fee?

It depends on what you need. Some clients come to us only for design, drawings and 3D views; others want design and execution together. We explain how the design work is charged in the proposal we send after the first visit, before you commit to anything.

How do payments work?

Payments are linked to clear stages of work, agreed in writing before the project starts. You always know what each payment covers, and the estimate you approved is the reference for any change.

Timelines & process

How long does a home interior project take?

As a rough guide, design and 3D approval for a full home take a few weeks, and site work for a complete 2BHK or 3BHK usually takes a few months. The real timeline depends on civil changes, the amount of carpentry and material lead times, and we put an agreed schedule in writing before work begins.

What are the steps from first call to handover?

Six steps: consultation and site measurement, space planning, 3D design, itemised estimation, execution on site, and handover with a snag list and deep clean. You can read each step in detail on our process page.

Can I see my home in 3D before work starts?

Yes. Every room is shown as photo-real 3D views with the actual laminates, fabrics and lights. You can ask for changes until you are happy, and work on site starts only after you approve the design.

Vastu & design

Do you design according to Vastu Shastra?

Yes, if you want it. We can plan the entrance, kitchen and cooking direction, master bedroom, pooja space and heavy storage in line with Vastu principles. Where a principle conflicts with a practical need, we show you the options side by side. If you already work with a Vastu consultant, we are happy to follow their guidance.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Often, yes. We measure the pieces you want to keep and plan the new layout, colours and lighting around them, so they look intended rather than left over.

Do you take on single rooms or only full homes?

Both. We design and build complete homes and offices, and also single rooms, modular kitchens and wardrobes. Tell us what you have in mind and we will suggest the right scope.

Materials & warranty

What materials do you use for kitchens and wardrobes?

We suggest boards and finishes for the job: moisture-resistant boards where there is water, durable laminates or veneers on surfaces that get daily use, and quality hinges and channels. You see and approve real samples in the studio, and every material is named in your estimate.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Manufacturer warranties on boards, hardware, appliances and fittings are passed on to you with the paperwork at handover. The terms of our own workmanship warranty are written into the agreement before work starts, so you know exactly what is covered.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based on SG Highway in Bodakdev and work across Ahmedabad, including areas such as Bodakdev, Thaltej, Satellite, Prahlad Nagar, Bopal, South Bopal and Gota. For projects outside the city, call us and we will tell you what is possible.
